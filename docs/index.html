The moves

Menu bar only

No Dock icon. The phase and a countdown sit up top, out of your way.

Three cues

Different sounds for stand up, sit down and stretch, picked from the macOS system sounds.

Out loud

Optional spoken reminders. One toggle in the menu if the office isn't ready.

Teams-aware

In a Microsoft Teams meeting it stays quiet: no speech, but you still get the banner and the chime.

English and French

French (France) is built in, picked up from your system language, with a manual override.

Starts at login

A launchd LaunchAgent starts it with your Mac. Nothing to remember.

It nags. It really nags.

Ignore a switch and the menu bar face gets more upset the longer you stay put. One click clears it.

Get your workout

  1. Grab the repo.

    git clone https://github.com/jessicadowney/PostureCoach.git cd PostureCoach

  2. Drop the app in Applications.

    mv PostureCoach.app /Applications/

  3. Run the installer. If Gatekeeper blocks a double-click, use Terminal.

    bash "Install PostureCoach.command"

Needs macOS 10.13+ and Python 3. The installer finds Python, installs the rumps menu bar library, and registers the login item.

Cool-down

What does the installer change?

It installs rumps for your Python 3, pins that interpreter in the app launcher, and adds one LaunchAgent so the app starts at login. It's a short shell script in the repo, so read it first.

Why does macOS block the installer?

The app isn't signed or notarized yet, so Gatekeeper is cautious. Running it with bash in Terminal gets around the prompt.

Is this medical advice?

No. It's a movement reminder. If pain is severe, radiates down your leg, causes numbness or weakness, or lasts more than a couple of weeks, see a clinician or physical therapist.
